IDEA VISUAL STYLE DEVELOPMENT CHALLENGES ITERATIONS

Idea

A quiet mystery about the stories people leave behind, told through the eyes of someone who cannot move on.

Visual style

Pixel-inspired environments and soft, expressive characters keep the world intimate and a little uncanny.

Development

I built the core interaction loop around exploring rooms, collecting clues, and choosing which memories to trust.

Challenges

The biggest challenge was balancing a readable interface with a world that should feel hazy and emotionally unresolved.

Iterations

Each pass tightened the pacing, clarified the clue system, and gave the visual language more room to breathe.

CONCLUSION

CONCLUSION

CONCLUSION

Before I Fade is a small story about memory, guilt, and the strange comfort of being remembered.
